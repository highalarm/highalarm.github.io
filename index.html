What is High Alarm?

High Alarm is a free Android alarm clock app made for people who sleep through a normal alarm. You set an alarm, pick one or more challenges, and the ringing stops only once your mind and body are awake. Even snooze asks for a challenge first.

Features

  • 30 wake-up challenges for your brain and your body
  • Loud alarm sounds with a volume boost up to 150%
  • Wake Up Check: fall back asleep and the alarm rings again
  • Cheating prevention, so the alarm is hard to skip
  • Sleep recorder for snoring and sleep talk
  • A short morning routine with a daily streak

Wake-up challenges

Maths, Advanced Maths, QR Code, Rewrite, Tap, Charger, Clock Reading, Lights On, Steps, Maze, Memory, Shake, Spell Bee, Puzzle, Scan Sink, Push Up, Squat, Scan Object, Reverse Typing, Audio Memory, Balance Test, Safe Code, Control Panel, Color Word, Smile Check, Say it Aloud, Cup Shuffle, Gold Zone, Flash Count and Dance.

Questions

Is High Alarm free?
Yes. High Alarm is free on Google Play. Some extra challenges and features are part of High Alarm PRO.
Which phones does High Alarm work on?
High Alarm works on Android phones running Android 7.0 or newer.
