How it works

What is High Alarm?

High Alarm is a free Android alarm clock app made for people who sleep through a normal alarm. You set an alarm, pick one or more challenges, and the ringing stops only once your mind and body are awake. Even snooze asks for a challenge first.

  1. The alarm ringsLoud, and it keeps going.
  2. You finish a challengeA sum, a QR code, a few steps or any challenge you picked.
  3. You're upFall back asleep and Wake Up Check rings again.

FAQ

Questions

Is High Alarm free?

Yes. High Alarm is free on Google Play. Some extra challenges and features are part of High Alarm PRO.

How do I turn off the alarm?

Finish the wake-up challenge you picked for that alarm, like a maths question, a QR code scan or a few steps. The alarm stops once it's done.

Can I snooze High Alarm?

Yes, but snooze asks for a challenge first, so you can't snooze half asleep.

Which phones does High Alarm work on?

High Alarm works on Android phones running Android 7.0 or newer.

Where can I download High Alarm?

Search "High Alarm" or "HighAlarm" on Google Play.
